## 🎯 Overview & Exam Pattern

The **C-DAC Common Admission Test (C-CAT)** is conducted twice a year (typically in January and July) for admission to C-DAC's Post Graduate Diploma / Certificate Programmes.

| Feature | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Exam Mode** | Computer-Based Test (CBT) |
| **Medium** | English |
| **Sections** | Category II (Sections A + B) or Category III (Sections A + B + C) |
| **Questions per Section** | 50 Objective-Type Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) |
| **Duration per Section** | 60 minutes (1 hour) per section |
| **Maximum Marks** | 150 marks per section (50 questions × 3 marks) |
| **Marking Scheme** | **+3** marks for correct answer, **-1** mark for wrong answer, **0** for unattempted |

## ⚠️ Critical Ranking & Section Qualification Rules

Understanding the official C-DAC ranking criteria is vital when planning your preparation:

1. **Mandatory Section-Wise Qualification:**
   - Candidates must qualify in **every individual section** required for their chosen category (Sections A and B for Category II; Sections A, B, and C for Category III).
2. **The Zero / Negative Marks Rule (Automatic Disqualification):**
   - Candidates with the lowest 10% performance in any section will not qualify for ranking.
   - **If a candidate scores zero or negative marks (≤ 0) in ANY individual section, they will NOT be awarded any rank in that category**, even if they score exceptionally high marks in the other section(s).
3. **Balanced Preparation is Essential:**
   - You cannot rely solely on Section A or Section B. Scoring well across all required sections is mandatory to obtain a valid rank and qualify for counseling.
4. **Tie-Breaker Mechanism:**
   - If two or more candidates obtain the same total marks, the candidate with higher marks in **Section A** is awarded the higher rank.
   - If Section A marks are also tied, the candidate with the higher accuracy ratio (`correct answers / attempted questions`) in Section B (or Section C for Category III) is awarded the higher rank.

## 💡 Preparation Strategy & Insights

1. **Ensure Sectional Balance:** Because scoring zero or negative marks in any individual section results in disqualification from ranking, allocate study effort across all required sections rather than focusing on just one.
2. **Deep-Dive into C Fundamentals & Output Questions:** Section B heavily tests C programming intricacies—such as pointer arithmetic, operator precedence, scope/storage classes, and dynamic memory behavior.
3. **Negative Marking Caution:** With a `-1` penalty for wrong answers, guessing blindly can quickly drop your score into negative territory or lower your percentile. Eliminate improbable choices before answering.
4. **Take Timed Mock Drills:** Practice solving full 50-question sets in timed 60-minute blocks to build comfort with exam pressure and test navigation.
5. **Review Core CS Theory:** For OS, Networking, and COA, focus on foundational definitions, scheduling algorithms, paging formulas, and cache structures.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<details>
<summary><b>1. What is the educational eligibility for PG-DAC?</b></summary>
<br>
Candidates typically require a Graduate in Engineering (10+2+4 or 10+3+3 years) in IT, Computer Science, Electronics, Telecommunications, Electrical, or Instrumentation; OR an MCA / Post Graduate Degree in Engineering Sciences / Computer Applications. Please verify exact branch criteria in the latest official C-DAC admission booklet.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2. How does the counseling and seat allocation process work?</b></summary>
<br>
Following the declaration of C-CAT ranks, candidates participate in online counseling by selecting and locking in their preferred centers and courses. Allocations are processed in successive rounds strictly based on rank, course preferences, and category-wise seat availability.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3. Are calculators allowed during the C-CAT exam?</b></summary>
<br>
No physical calculators are permitted. If enabled by the test platform, an on-screen basic virtual calculator may be accessible, though most questions test core conceptual logic and basic arithmetic.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4. Which C-DAC training centers are the most sought-after?</b></summary>
<br>
Historically, <b>CDAC ACTS Pune</b> and <b>CDAC Bengaluru</b> are the most preferred centers due to their extensive industry alumni network, infrastructure, and strong placement track records.
</details>
