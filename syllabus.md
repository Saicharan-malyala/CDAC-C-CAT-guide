## 🗂️ Course-Wise C-CAT Categories

The C-CAT is divided into three test sections. Depending on your chosen PG Diploma courses, candidates register for either **Category II** or **Category III**:

| Category | Sections to Appear | Target Courses Offered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Category II** | **Section A + B** | • **PGCP-AC / PG-DAC** (Advanced Computing)<br>• **PGCP-BDA / PG-DBDA** (Big Data Analytics)<br>• **PGCP-AI / PG-DAI** (Artificial Intelligence)<br>• **PGCP-ITISS / PG-DITISS** (IT Infrastructure, Systems & Security)<br>• **PGCP-MC / PG-DMC** (Mobile Computing)<br>• **PGCP-ASSD** (Advanced Secure Software Development)<br>• **PGCP-HPCSA / PG-DHPCSA** (HPC System Administration)<br>• **PGCP-FBD** (FinTech & Blockchain Development)<br>• **PGCP-CSF / PG-DCSF** (Cyber Security & Forensics)<br>• **PGCP-RAT** (Robotics & Allied Technologies) |

## 📘 Detailed Syllabus Breakdown

### 🔹 SECTION A (60 Minutes - 50 MCQs, 150 Marks)
*Mandatory for Category II and Category III. Focuses on English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, and Computer Fundamentals.*

#### 1. English (Verbal Ability)
* **Grammar:** Tenses, Prepositions, Articles, Active & Passive Voice, Spotting Errors, Sentence Correction.
* **Vocabulary & Comprehension:** Synonyms & Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, One-word Substitution, Reading Comprehension.
* **Recommended Book:** *High School English Grammar & Composition (Wren & Martin)*.

#### 2. Quantitative Aptitude & Reasoning
* **Quantitative Aptitude:** Number Systems, Fractions & Decimals, Averages, Problems on Ages, Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Time & Work, Time, Speed & Distance, Permutations & Combinations, Probability.
* **Logical Reasoning:** Series Completion (Numbers, Alphabets), Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Seating Arrangement (Linear & Circular), Puzzles, Syllogisms.
* **Recommended Books:** *Quantitative Aptitude Fully Solved (R.S. Aggarwal)*, *Quantitative Aptitude (M. Tyra)*, *Barron's New GRE*.

#### 3. Computer Fundamentals & Concepts of Programming
* **Hardware & Memory:** Generations of Computers, RAM, ROM, Cache Memory, Virtual Memory, Secondary Storage Devices.
* **OS & Number Systems:** Basics of Operating Systems, Number System Conversions (Binary, Decimal, Octal, Hexadecimal).
* **Networks & Security:** Networking basics (LAN, WAN, TCP/IP, OSI Layers), Internet & Cybersecurity fundamentals.
* **Recommended Book:** *Foundations of Computing (Pradeep K. Sinha & Priti Sinha)*.

---

### 🔸 SECTION B (60 Minutes - 50 MCQs, 150 Marks)
*Mandatory for Category II & III. Focuses on Computer Programming, Data Structures, OOP, Operating Systems, Networking, and Basics of Big Data/AI.*

#### 1. C Programming
* **Fundamentals:** Data Types, Constants, Variables, Operators (Arithmetic, Bitwise, Logical, Relational).
* **Control Flow:** Decision Control (if-else, nested-if, switch-case), Loop Control (for, while, do-while, break, continue).
* **Functions & Storage Classes:** Recursion, Storage Classes (`auto`, `register`, `static`, `extern`).
* **Pointers & Memory:** Pointer Arithmetic, Double Pointers, Dynamic Memory Allocation (`malloc`, `calloc`, `realloc`, `free`), Structures, Unions, and Bitfields.
* **Recommended Books:** *The C Programming Language (Kernighan & Ritchie)*, *Let Us C (Yashavant Kanetkar)*.

#### 2. Data Structures
* **Linear Data Structures:** Arrays, Singly Linked Lists, Doubly Linked Lists, Circular Linked Lists, Stacks (LIFO), Queues (FIFO), Circular Queues.
* **Non-Linear Data Structures:** Binary Trees, Binary Search Trees (BST), AVL Trees, Graphs (BFS and DFS traversals).
* **Algorithms:** Sorting (Bubble, Selection, Insertion, Merge, Quick, Heap sort) and Searching (Linear & Binary search).
* **Recommended Book:** *Data Structures Through C in Depth (S. K. Srivastava)*.

#### 3. Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) Concepts using C++
* **Core Concepts:** Classes, Objects, Abstraction, Encapsulation, Data Hiding, Constructors & Destructors.
* **Advanced OOP:** Inheritance, Polymorphism (Function & Operator Overloading, Virtual Functions, Pure Virtual Functions, Abstract Classes), Access Specifiers, Templates, Exception Handling.
* **Recommended Book:** *Test Your C++ Skills (Yashavant Kanetkar)*.

#### 4. Operating Systems & Networking
* **Process Management:** Process Lifecycle, Threads, CPU Scheduling Algorithms (FCFS, SJF, Round Robin, Priority).
* **Concurrency & Synchronization:** Semaphores, Mutex, Critical Section, Deadlocks (Conditions, Banker's Algorithm, Prevention).
* **Memory Management:** Paging, Segmentation, Virtual Memory, Page Replacement Algorithms (FIFO, LRU, Optimal).
* **Networking:** OSI and TCP/IP Reference Models, IP Addressing, Subnetting, Routing basics, Common Protocols (HTTP, DNS, TCP, UDP).
* **Recommended Books:** *Operating System Concepts (Silberschatz, Galvin, Gagne)*, *Data Communications and Networking (Behrouz A. Forouzan)*.

#### 5. Basics of Big Data & Artificial Intelligence
* **Big Data:** 5 V's of Big Data, Distributed Computing fundamentals, Hadoop Ecosystem (HDFS & MapReduce overview).
* **Artificial Intelligence:** AI definitions, Turing Test, Types of Machine Learning (Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement Learning).
* **Recommended Books:** *Fundamentals of Data Engineering (Joe Reis, Matt Housley)*, *Artificial Intelligence for Dummies (John Paul Mueller, Luca Massaron)*.

---

### 🔺 SECTION C (60 Minutes - 50 MCQs, 150 Marks)
*Mandatory for Category III. Focuses on Digital Electronics, Microprocessors, and Computer Organization & Architecture.*

#### 1. Digital Electronics
* **Number Systems & Codes:** Binary, Octal, Hex, BCD, Gray Code, Excess-3.
* **Combinational Circuits:** Boolean Algebra simplification, Karnaugh Maps (K-Maps), Logic Gates, Adders, Subtractors, Multiplexers, Decoders, Encoders.
* **Sequential Circuits:** Latches & Flip-Flops (SR, JK, D, T), Excitation tables, Master-Slave Flip-Flops, Synchronous & Asynchronous Counters, Shift Registers.
* **Recommended Books:** *Digital Design (M. Morris Mano)*, *Modern Digital Electronics (R. P. Jain)*, *Digital Design: Principles & Practices (John Wakerly)*.

#### 2. Computer Organization & Architecture (COA)
* **Processor & Control Unit:** Machine Instructions, Addressing Modes, Instruction Cycles, Hardwired vs. Microprogrammed Control Units.
* **Memory Hierarchy & I/O:** Cache Memory Mapping (Direct, Associative, Set-Associative), Virtual Memory, DMA (Direct Memory Access), Interrupts, Pipelining Hazards & Speedup formulas.
* **Recommended Book:** *Computer Organization and Architecture (William Stallings)*.

#### 3. Microprocessors (8085 / 8086)
* **8085 Microprocessor:** Architecture, Pin Configuration, Register Array, Instruction Set, Addressing Modes, Timing Diagrams, Interrupts.
* **8086 Microprocessor:** Internal Architecture (BIU & EU), Memory Segmentation (Segment:Offset), 8086 Registers, Flag Register, Basic Assembly concepts.
* **Recommended Books:** *Microprocessor Architecture, Programming and Applications with the 8085 (Ramesh Gaonkar)*, *The Intel Microprocessors (Barry B. Brey)*.
